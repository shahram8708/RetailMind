RetailMind Feature Deep Dive

Each autonomous mission is purpose built for high traffic Indian malls with real-time operational complexity.

Feature 1

Inventory Watch Engine

What It Does: Continuously monitors SKU level demand, zone footfall, and supplier lead times to predict stockout risk scores in real time.

Why It Matters: Prevents high margin product stockouts during peak windows and reduces emergency replenishment costs.

Workflow
  1. Ingest sales velocity and stock snapshots every 15 minutes.
  2. Compute SKU Risk Score with seasonality and footfall context.
  3. Trigger approval-ready restock recommendations.
Input Data
  • POS transactions
  • Inventory levels
  • Supplier SLA and lead time
  • Zone wise foot traffic
Output Generated
  • SRS based restock alerts
  • Priority queue by revenue impact
  • Supplier action recommendations

Feature 2

Campaign Activation Engine

What It Does: Detects promotional opportunity windows using traffic surges, weather context, and shopper intent patterns.

Why It Matters: Improves campaign conversion while protecting budget from low probability activations.

Workflow
  1. Track target zone movement and conversion trends.
  2. Score campaign opportunity in real time.
  3. Auto activate or request approval with suggested copy.
Input Data
  • Footfall by hour and zone
  • Weather and event context
  • Historical campaign ROI
  • Tenant inventory readiness
Output Generated
  • Opportunity score and channel recommendation
  • Campaign copy drafts
  • Post campaign conversion analytics

Feature 3

Facility Intelligence Engine

What It Does: Watches sensor telemetry across mission critical equipment and flags rising anomaly trends before failure.

Why It Matters: Avoids expensive emergency repairs, improves uptime, and protects mall customer experience.

Workflow
  1. Collect vibration, temperature, and electrical readings.
  2. Compute failure propensity score for each asset.
  3. Generate preventive work order recommendations.
Input Data
  • IoT sensor streams
  • Equipment maintenance history
  • Utilization and age factors
  • Anomaly z score trends
Output Generated
  • FPS alert feed
  • Work order priorities
  • Risk scored maintenance plan
Sensor Data
->
Anomaly Model
->
Work Order
Feature 4

Shopper Navigation Agent

What It Does: Interprets natural language shopper queries and returns exact store and product guidance in seconds.

Why It Matters: Reduces walk aways, increases conversion, and gives mall operators a measurable service advantage.

Workflow
  1. Capture shopper intent from query text.
  2. Match inventory across tenants by relevance.
  3. Return zone, floor, and product level guidance.
Input Data
  • Shopper search text
  • Live inventory index
  • Mall zone and floor map
  • Product attribute metadata
Output Generated
  • Relevant store shortlist
  • Fast route hints
  • Interaction and conversion insights
